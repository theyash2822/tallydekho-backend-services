# TallyDekho — Mobile App Navigation

## Bottom Navigation Tabs
1. **Dashboard** — KPI summary, sync status, quick actions
2. **Sales** — Sales invoices, orders, credit notes, delivery notes, quotations
3. **Purchase** — Purchase invoices, orders, debit notes
4. **Stocks** — Inventory overview, stock detail, warehouses
5. **Reports** — Financial reports, compliance, AI Insights, daybook

## Dashboard
- Shows: Total Revenue, Outstanding, Cash Balance, Stock Value (KPIs)
- Pairing banner appears if not connected to Tally
- Offline badge if last sync was too long ago
- Tap any KPI card to drill down into detail

## Sales Module
- Sales Register: list of all sales vouchers
- Tap + to create a new sales invoice
- Filter by: date range, voucher type, party
- Tap a voucher to view details or share as PDF

## Purchase Module
- Purchase Register: list of all purchase vouchers
- Tap + to create new purchase entry
- Same filter options as Sales

## Stocks Tab
- Default: All stock items with qty and value
- Switch views: On-Hand, Low Stock, Negative, Warehouses
- Tap any item for full transaction history

## Reports Tab
- Financial: P&L, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, Bill Ageing
- Compliance: GST, E-Invoice, E-Way Bill, Other Taxes, Audit Trail
- Operations: Daybook, Cash Register
- AI: AI Insights

## Header Controls
- Company switcher (top left): switch between companies
- FY switcher (top right): switch financial year
- These affect ALL screens simultaneously

## Searching
- Search icon available on most list screens
- Type party name, voucher number, or item name

## PDF Generation
- Tap Share icon on any voucher → generates PDF
- PDF includes: company logo, GST details, line items, totals, QR code
- Format depends on Settings → Voucher Config

## Settings
- Access via profile icon or Settings tab
- Profile, Security/2FA, Company Info, Notifications, Tax, Tally Sync, License
