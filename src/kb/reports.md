# TallyDekho — Reports

## Available Reports

### Financial Reports
- **P&L (Profit & Loss)**: Revenue vs expenses, gross profit, net profit by FY
- **Balance Sheet**: Assets, liabilities, equity as of period end
- **Trial Balance**: All ledger groups with Dr/Cr balances — must balance

### Compliance Reports
- GSTR-1, 3B, 4, 9, 9C (GST returns)
- E-Invoice status (IRN pending / generated)
- E-Way Bill status
- Other Taxes (TDS, TCS, VAT, Cess)
- Audit Trail

### Operational Reports
- Daybook: All vouchers across all types by date
- Bill Ageing: Outstanding receivables by age bucket (0-30, 31-60, 60+ days)
- Cash Register: Daily cash-in / cash-out
- AI Insights: Analytics, forecasts, recommendations

## Accessing Reports
1. Tap Reports tab from bottom navigation
2. Select the report type from the list
3. Use FY selector at top to change financial year
4. Use date range picker where available

## Financial Year Switching
- All reports respect the selected FY
- Change FY from the dropdown in the header
- Historical FY data is available for all synced years

## P&L Report
- Shows: Total Revenue, COGS (opening + purchases - closing stock), Gross Profit, Expenses, Net Profit
- Tap any line to see ledger-level breakdown
- Opening/Closing stock pulled from Tally stock valuation XML

## Trial Balance
- Groups ledgers by account type (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expenses)
- Total Dr must equal total Cr — shows "Difference in Opening Balances" if not
- Tap a group to see ledger details

## Daybook
- Shows all vouchers for a selected period
- Filter: All / My Entries (vouchers you created)
- Collapsible month sections
- Multi-select for export

## Cash Register
- Shows daily cash movement (receipts and payments)
- Useful for cash flow tracking
- Accessible from KPI screens or Reports tab

## Bill Ageing
- Shows outstanding receivables grouped by age
- Age buckets: 0-30 days, 31-60 days, 61+ days
- Tap a party to see their individual bills
