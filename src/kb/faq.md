# TallyDekho — Frequently Asked Questions

## Login & Account
Q: How do I login?
A: TallyDekho uses OTP-only login. Enter your registered mobile number → receive OTP on WhatsApp/SMS → enter OTP to login.

Q: I didn't receive my OTP?
A: Check if WhatsApp is installed. Wait 30 seconds and try resend. Check your network connection.

Q: Can I use the same account on multiple devices?
A: Yes — login on any device. All data syncs via the cloud backend.

Q: I forgot my PIN?
A: Go to Settings → Security → Forgot PIN → verify via OTP to reset.

## Data & Sync
Q: Is my data real or demo data?
A: After pairing with Tally, all data is real. Before pairing, demo data is shown.

Q: How often does data sync?
A: Auto-sync every 10-15 minutes when Desktop Agent is running. You can trigger manual sync anytime.

Q: Can I use TallyDekho without Tally Prime?
A: You can create vouchers in TallyDekho, but data won't sync without Tally Prime pairing.

Q: Does TallyDekho work offline?
A: Yes — you can view cached data and create entries offline. They sync when connection restores.

## Vouchers & Entries
Q: Can I edit Tally vouchers from the app?
A: No — vouchers created in Tally are read-only in TallyDekho. Edit in Tally, then resync.

Q: What is an Optional Entry?
A: A draft entry saved in TallyDekho but not posted to Tally until you approve it. Shows with a purple "Draft" badge.

Q: Can I delete a voucher from the app?
A: Only vouchers created in TallyDekho (not yet posted) can be deleted from the app.

## PDF & Sharing
Q: How do I share an invoice as PDF?
A: Open any voucher → tap the Share icon → PDF is generated with your company letterhead.

Q: How do I add my company logo to PDFs?
A: Settings → Company Information → Upload Logo → Save.

Q: Can I customise the invoice format?
A: Yes — Settings → Voucher Config → choose Format 1, 2, or 3. Enable QR code and Terms & Conditions.

## Reports
Q: Why is my P&L showing wrong figures?
A: Trigger a fresh sync. Make sure the correct Financial Year is selected. Opening/Closing stock values depend on Tally stock valuation settings.

Q: Trial Balance doesn't balance?
A: A "Difference in Opening Balances" row is shown if there's a discrepancy. This usually indicates opening balance data is incomplete in Tally.

Q: How do I view a previous year's data?
A: Use the FY selector in the header to switch financial years. All reports update for the selected year.
